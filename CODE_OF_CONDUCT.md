# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We are committed to making participation in this project a respectful,
constructive experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate, respectful, and collaborative.
- Critique evidence and ideas, not people.
- Accept correction and distinguish verified facts from hypotheses.
- Respect project scope, intellectual-property boundaries, and privacy.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, doxxing, threats, and repeated
disruptive conduct are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Contact the project maintainer privately through GitHub to report a concern.
Reports will be reviewed and handled with discretion. The maintainer may edit
or remove contributions and may restrict participation when necessary to keep
the project safe and constructive.

This is a project-specific, concise code of conduct; it is inspired by the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), but does not
claim formal adoption of a particular version.
